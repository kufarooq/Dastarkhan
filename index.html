Our story

From a roadside grill to your family table

It started with one charcoal grill and a tray of seekh kababs beside the G.T. Road. Travellers stopped for a single kabab and stayed for a second, and soon the grill had a karahi next to it, then a pot of biryani, then a few tables under a tin roof.

Today that roadside grill is Karachi Restaurant, a family restaurant near Govt Girls College, but the routine has not changed. The coals are lit before noon, masalas are ground fresh every morning, and every karahi is cooked to order while you wait.

A dastarkhwan is the cloth spread for a shared meal, and that is still what we offer: generous plates, fair prices and a seat for everyone, whether you stop by for a quick plate of biryani or bring the whole family for dinner.
